# Manha — Grade 4 Summer Prep Plan

**Goal:** Bridge the gap between Grade 3 and Grade 4 so Manha is ahead of her class in Fall.
**Timeframe:** 2 months (8 weeks)
**Schedule:** 4 days per week, 2 subjects per day
**Total sessions:** 32 days × 2 subjects = 64 subject slots

---

## 1. Why These Subjects Matter in Grade 4

| Subject | Grade 4 Big Leap | What Manha Must Preview |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------------|
| **Math** | Numbers up to 1,000,000; multi-digit multiplication/division; fractions with more denominators; decimals; angles; multi-step word problems | Multiplicative comparison, area models, fraction equivalence, decimal basics, protractor use |
| **ELA** | Close reading with text evidence; comparing texts; opinion/informative writing with paragraphs; Greek/Latin roots; figurative language | Summarizing with evidence, paragraph structure, root words, similes/metaphors |
| **Science** | Energy, light, Earth's features, waves; engineering design process | Energy conversions, light reflection, rock layers/fossils, sound waves |
| **Social Studies** | NYS geography, Native Americans, colonial/revolutionary NY, government, immigration | NYS map, Haudenosaunee/Algonquian basics, levels of government, Ellis Island |

## 3. 8-Week Session Schedule

### Week 1 — Diagnostic & Foundations
| Day | Subject A | Subject B |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | **Math:** Place value to 1,000,000; comparing/rounding | **Science:** What is energy? Forms of energy |
| 2 | **ELA:** Close reading practice — finding text evidence | **Social Studies:** NYS geography basics |
| 3 | **Math:** Multiplicative comparison (× as "times as many") | **ELA:** Summary vs. central idea review |
| 4 | **Science:** Energy transfers — sound, light, heat, electric | **Social Studies:** Maps: physical, political, thematic |

### Week 2 — Multi-Digit Operations
| Day | Subject A | Subject B |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | **Math:** Multi-digit addition/subtraction algorithms | **Science:** Energy conversions intro |
| 2 | **ELA:** Text structure — sequence & cause/effect | **Social Studies:** Native American groups: Haudenosaunee & Algonquian |
| 3 | **Math:** Multiply 4-digit × 1-digit with area models | **ELA:** Writing paragraphs with topic sentence |
| 4 | **Science:** Designing devices that convert energy | **Social Studies:** How geography shaped Native American life |

### Week 3 — Multiplication & Division
| Day | Subject A | Subject B |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | **Math:** 2-digit × 2-digit multiplication | **Science:** Vision and light — how we see |
| 2 | **ELA:** Opinion writing — claim + reasons | **Social Studies:** European explorers: Verrazano, Hudson, Champlain |
| 3 | **Math:** Division with remainders (4-digit ÷ 1-digit) | **ELA:** Using linking words in writing |
| 4 | **Science:** Light reflection and eye structures | **Social Studies:** New Netherland → New York colony |

### Week 4 — Fractions
| Day | Subject A | Subject B |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | **Math:** Fraction equivalence with models | **Science:** Animal structures and survival |
| 2 | **ELA:** Informative/explanatory writing | **Social Studies:** Colonial life in New York |
| 3 | **Math:** Comparing fractions; common denominators | **ELA:** Greek & Latin roots and affixes |
| 4 | **Science:** How animals process information from senses | **Social Studies:** American Revolution in NY — Loyalists & Patriots |

### Week 5 — Fractions & Decimals
| Day | Subject A | Subject B |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | **Math:** Add/subtract fractions with like denominators | **Science:** Earth's features — rock layers & fossils |
| 2 | **ELA:** Figurative language — similes & metaphors | **Social Studies:** Branches of government (federal/state/local) |
| 3 | **Math:** Mixed numbers; multiply whole number × fraction | **ELA:** Multiple-meaning words & context clues |
| 4 | **Science:** Weathering, erosion, deposition | **Social Studies:** Brooklyn/Kings County local government |

### Week 6 — Measurement & Geometry
| Day | Subject A | Subject B |
|-----|-----------|-----------|

### Week 7 — Angles & Advanced Reading
| Day | Subject A | Subject B |

### Week 8 — Capstone & Review
| Day | Subject A | Subject B |
|-----|-----------|-----------|

## 4. Key Vocabulary to Pre-Teach

**Math:** multiplicative comparison, factor, multiple, prime, composite, equivalent fraction, mixed number, decimal, tenths, hundredths, protractor, degrees, area, perimeter, conversion

**ELA:** evidence, inference, theme, central idea, text structure, claim, reason, counterclaim, opinion, informative, narrative, simile, metaphor, idiom, proverb, root word, affix

**Science:** energy transfer, energy conversion, circuit, current, renewable resource, reflection, structure and function, sense receptor, weathering, erosion, deposition, fossil, rock layer, wave, amplitude, wavelength

**Social Studies:** Haudenosaunee, Algonquian, longhouse, colony, explorer, revolution, patriot, loyalist, government, legislative, executive, judicial, citizen, rights, responsibilities, immigration, Ellis Island, Erie Canal
