Terms of use

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Educational purpose

LearnFlow is provided as a tutoring and practice workspace. Content may be incomplete, under development, or marked “Coming soon.” Students should follow their teacher’s directions and verify important schoolwork with a qualified adult.

Responsible use

  • Use the site only for lawful educational activity.
  • Do not enter another person’s private information.
  • Do not attempt to bypass school, device, or network filters.
  • Do not upload harmful code, attack the service, impersonate another student, or interfere with someone else’s work.
  • Keep any access PIN private and do not reuse an important password.

Access and availability

Access may be limited by a school, family, device, browser, network administrator, or hosting provider. We do not promise that the site will be available on every network or that every lesson will always be error-free.

Ownership and feedback

LearnFlow’s original name, layout, code, and original lesson materials remain with their respective owner. Do not copy, redistribute, or modify private lesson materials without permission. Suggestions and corrections may be sent through the teacher or site administrator’s approved channel.

Changes

These terms may change as the tutoring workspace grows. The date above shows when this page was last reviewed. Continued use after an update means the revised terms apply.
