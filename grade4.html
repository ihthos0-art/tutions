A Dog Named Biscuit

Biscuit was a small brown dog who lived on Oak Street. Every day, he waited by the front door for his owner, Lily, to come home from school. When Lily walked in, Biscuit wagged his tail so fast that his whole body shook. Lily always laughed and gave him a big hug.

One day, Lily came home feeling sad. She had done badly on a spelling test. But when she saw Biscuit waiting for her, she felt a little better. Biscuit licked her hand and curled up next to her on the couch. By dinnertime, Lily was smiling again.

After the Storm

“Storms are loud,” Maya said, “but they always end.” She read him a book until the rain slowed down. When the sun came back out, Theo ran to the window. There was a rainbow stretching across the sky. “You were right,” he said. Maya smiled.

The Snowy Day

Peter woke up and looked out his window. Everything was covered in snow. The trees wore white hats. The cars looked like soft lumps of dough. Even the fence had a thick coat of frosting on top. Peter could not wait to go outside.

He put on his snowsuit, his hat, and his boots. His mother helped him with his scarf. Then he ran out the door and into the quiet street. The snow crunched under his boots. He made footprints in every direction, turning around to look at the pattern they made.

Peter tried to make a snowball, but the snow was too powdery. It fell apart in his hands. So he lay down on his back and moved his arms and legs up and down. When he stood up, there was a perfect angel in the snow. He smiled. It was the best snow day he could remember.

Question 1 — Central Idea

What is the most important idea in this story? Write it in one sentence.

Question 2 — Supporting Detail

Find one detail from the story that shows Peter is having fun. Use words from the passage.

Question 3 — Make a Connection

What is your favorite thing to do on a snowy day? Write one sentence.
