The Brave Little Bee

Read the story, then write a summary below

Read the story

Bina was a small bee who lived in a yellow hive at the edge of a meadow. Every morning she flew out to collect nectar from flowers. But Bina was afraid of the big, dark thunderclouds that sometimes rolled in across the sky. One day, a huge cloud covered the sun just as she was far from home.

Bina heard her friend Buzz call out from behind a daisy. “Don’t be scared! Just follow the wind home!” Bina took a deep breath and flew fast through the light rain. When she finally landed back at the hive, the other bees cheered. Bina smiled. She had been braver than she ever knew she could be.

Tell Your Teacher

Tell your teacher about this story. What happened? How did Bina feel at the end? Write 2–3 sentences.

Reading  ·  Grade 1

The Lost Kite

Read the story, then tell your teacher about it

Read the story

Lena had a red kite with a long white tail. She flew it in the park on a windy day. The wind blew hard and fast. The kite flew up and got stuck in a big tree. Lena pulled the string, but the kite would not come down.

Her dad came over and looked up at the tree. He threw a stick at the branch and shook it. The kite fell right into Lena’s hands! She laughed and gave her dad a big hug. They flew the kite again until the sun went down.

Tell Your Teacher

Tell your teacher about this story. What happened to the kite? How did Lena get it back? Write 2–3 sentences.

Reading  ·  Grade 1

The Hungry Cat

Read the story, then tell your teacher about it

Read the story

A gray cat named Pip was very hungry. She sniffed the air and smelled fish. She followed the smell down the road past the shops. The smell led her to a man sitting by the lake with a big bag of fish.

The man saw Pip and smiled. “Are you hungry, little cat?” he said. He gave her a small piece of fish. Pip ate it fast and purred very loudly. The man gave her one more piece. Pip walked home with a full belly and a happy heart.

Tell Your Teacher

Tell your teacher about this story. Where did Pip go? What did the man do? Write 2–3 sentences.

Reading  ·  Grade 1

The Small Frog

Read the story, then tell your teacher about it

Read the story

Finn was the smallest frog in the pond. All the big frogs could jump to the flat rock in the middle. Finn tried and tried but always fell into the water. He did not give up.

Every day Finn jumped a little more. His legs got stronger. One morning he took a big leap — and landed right on the rock! The big frogs went quiet. Then they all began to cheer. Finn sat on the rock and smiled the biggest smile of his life.

Tell Your Teacher

Tell your teacher about this story. What did Finn want to do? What happened at the end? Write 2–3 sentences.

Story 5

Reading  ·  Grade 1

The Blue Boat

Read the story, then tell your teacher about it

Read the story

Sam made a little boat out of blue paper. He took it outside and put it in a rain puddle. The boat floated on the water! Sam pushed it with his finger and it sailed across the puddle. He was so happy.

Then a big raindrop fell and the paper got wet. The boat slowly sank to the bottom of the puddle. Sam was sad for a moment. But he went inside and made a new one — this time with two layers of paper. His new boat was even better than the first.

Tell Your Teacher

Tell your teacher about this story. What did Sam make? What happened to it? Write 2–3 sentences.

Story 6

Reading  ·  Grade 1

The Bird and the Worm

Read the story, then tell your teacher about it

Read the story

A red bird named Ruby wanted a worm for her babies. She saw one sticking out of the dirt. She pulled and pulled but the worm held on tight. She hopped around and tried again. The worm still would not come out.

Ruby sat and thought of a new plan. She pecked the dirt next to the hole very fast. The worm got scared and jumped right out! Ruby grabbed it and flew back to her nest. Her babies were waiting. They all ate well that night.

Tell Your Teacher

Tell your teacher about this story. What did Ruby want? How did she finally get it? Write 2–3 sentences.

The Big Puddle

Nabila & Naviha  ·  Read the story, then tell your teacher about it

Read the story

It rained hard all night. In the morning there was a huge puddle in front of the school gate. All the kids walked around it to stay dry. But one boy ran right up to the puddle and jumped in with both feet. Water flew up everywhere.

His shoes were soaked. His socks were wet. His pants were dripping. His friend looked at him and shook her head. The boy looked down at his wet shoes. Then he started to laugh. “It was worth it!” he said. His friend started to laugh too. They both walked into school with big smiles on their faces.

Tell Your Teacher

Tell your teacher about this story. What did the boy do? What happened after? Write 2–3 sentences.
