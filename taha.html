The Friendly Cat

Every morning, a small orange cat sat on the front steps of Mr. Henderson's house. The cat did not belong to anyone. The neighbors called it Marmalade because of its bright color. Marmalade did not beg for food. It simply sat there, watching people walk by, and purred when anyone stopped to pet it.

One cold November morning, a little girl named Aisha was waiting for the school bus. She was shivering because she had forgotten her jacket. Marmalade walked over and rubbed against her leg. Aisha sat down on the steps and held the warm cat in her lap. By the time the bus came, Aisha was not cold anymore. She smiled and gave Marmalade one last pat before running to the bus.

That afternoon, Aisha asked her mother if they could leave a small blanket on the steps for the cat. Her mother agreed. From that day on, Aisha and Marmalade were friends. Every morning, the cat was there. And every morning, Aisha brought a little treat.

Question 1 — Central Idea

What is the most important idea in this story? Write it in one sentence.

Question 2 — Supporting Detail

Find one detail from the story that shows Marmalade is friendly. Use words from the passage.

Question 3 — Make a Connection

Do you have a pet, or do you know an animal in your neighborhood? Write one sentence about it.
