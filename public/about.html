About LearnFlow

A clear place for assigned practice and tutoring support.

What this website is for

LearnFlow is a student tutoring workspace for assigned practice. Each student has a simple learning page with four subject areas: Math, English, Social Studies, and Science. New lessons can be added by the teacher or site administrator as they are ready.

What students can do here

  • Open the page assigned to them.
  • Practice age-appropriate reading, writing, and math skills.
  • Use guided help to understand a question instead of receiving a copied answer.
  • Return to the same page from a school-approved device.

Designed for responsible school use

The site is intentionally focused on learning. It does not present itself as a social network, entertainment service, or official NYC Public Schools website. School and network administrators remain in control of access, filtering, and device rules.

LearnFlow is not affiliated with or endorsed by the New York City Department of Education. If a school network blocks the site, ask the school’s technology administrator to review the domain.
