The Lost Kite

Omar loved his bright red kite. On Saturday mornings, he ran to the empty field behind his building and let it fly high above the rooftops. One windy afternoon, a strong gust pulled the kite sideways. The string tangled around the branch of a tall oak tree. No matter how hard Omar pulled, the kite would not come free.

An old man named Mr. Karim was raking leaves nearby. He saw Omar looking up sadly at the tree. Without saying a word, Mr. Karim put down his rake and brought a long pole from his shed. He gently reached up and untangled the string. The kite floated back down into Omar’s hands.

Omar smiled and said thank you. Mr. Karim sat down on a bench and told Omar that he used to fly kites as a boy too. Omar sat beside him and they talked for a long time. After that day, Omar always stopped to say hello to Mr. Karim. The lost kite had brought them together, and they stayed friends.

Question 1 — Central Idea

What is the most important idea in this story? Write it in one sentence.

Question 2 — Supporting Detail

How did Mr. Karim help Omar? Use words from the passage in your answer.

Question 3 — Make a Connection

Write one sentence about a time someone helped you, or a time you helped someone else.
