A Dog Named Biscuit

Biscuit was a small brown dog who lived on Oak Street. Every day, he waited by the front door for his owner, Lily, to come home from school. When Lily walked in, Biscuit wagged his tail so fast that his whole body shook. Lily always laughed and gave him a big hug.

One day, Lily came home feeling sad. She had done badly on a spelling test. But when she saw Biscuit waiting for her, she felt a little better. Biscuit licked her hand and curled up next to her on the couch. By dinnertime, Lily was smiling again.

After the Storm

One afternoon, dark clouds rolled over the town. The wind began to blow and rain started to fall hard. Maya’s little brother, Theo, was scared. He ran to his room and hid under his blanket. Maya followed him and sat beside him on the bed.

“Storms are loud,” Maya said, “but they always end.” She read him a book until the rain slowed down. When the sun came back out, Theo ran to the window. There was a rainbow stretching across the sky. “You were right,” he said. Maya smiled.

The Big Race

Every year, the school held a race on Field Day. This year, Jamal wanted to win more than anything. He practiced running every day after school, even when he was tired. His older sister timed him with her phone and cheered him on from the sidewalk.

On the day of the race, Jamal ran as fast as he could. His legs hurt, but he kept going. He did not come in first. He came in third. When the race was over, his sister ran over to him. “That was your best time ever,” she said. Jamal was still breathing hard, but he smiled. He had done his best. That was enough.

The Lost Puppy

On the way home from school, Rosa found a small white puppy sitting alone near the park fence. It had no collar and no tags. Rosa picked it up and carried it home. She named it Snowball, gave it water, and made a soft bed out of an old blanket.

Rosa wanted to keep Snowball more than anything. But her mother helped her make a sign and put it up around the neighborhood. Two days later, a little boy named Sam knocked on their door. His eyes were red from crying. The puppy was his and had been missing for three days. Rosa handed Snowball over slowly. Sam hugged the dog tight and said thank you. It was hard to let go, but Rosa knew she had done the right thing.
