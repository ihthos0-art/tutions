The Brave Little Bee

Read the story, then write a summary below

Read the story

Bina was a small bee who lived in a yellow hive at the edge of a meadow. Every morning she flew out to collect nectar from flowers. But Bina was afraid of the big, dark thunderclouds that sometimes rolled in across the sky. One day, a huge cloud covered the sun just as she was far from home.

Bina heard her friend Buzz call out from behind a daisy. “Don’t be scared! Just follow the wind home!” Bina took a deep breath and flew fast through the light rain. When she finally landed back at the hive, the other bees cheered. Bina smiled. She had been braver than she ever knew she could be.

Summary

What happened in this story? How did Bina feel at the end? Write 2–3 sentences in your own words.

ELA Practice  ·  Grade 1

The Big Red Hat

Nabila & Naviha  ·  Read the story, then answer the questions below

Read the story

Max found a big red hat on the sidewalk. He put it on his head. But the hat was too big! It fell down over his eyes. He could not see. He walked into the couch. He walked into the door. He even sat on his dog Bingo by mistake!

Bingo was not hurt. He got up and grabbed the hat with his teeth. He pulled it right off Max’s head. Max could see again! He started to laugh. Bingo wagged his tail. Max said, “Good dog, Bingo!” Then Max put the big red hat on Bingo instead. It fit just right. They went for a walk, and everyone who saw them smiled.

Question 1 — What Happened?

What happened when Max put the hat on? Write 1–2 sentences.

Question 2 — How Did Bingo Help?

How did Bingo help Max? Write 1–2 sentences.

Question 3 — How Did Max Feel?

How did Max feel at the end of the story? Write one sentence.
