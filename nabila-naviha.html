The Rainy Day

Read the story, then write a summary below

Read the story

Zara looked out the window and frowned. It was raining again. She had wanted to ride her bike to the park, but the sky was gray and the streets were wet. She sat on the couch and did not know what to do.

Then her dad came in with two pieces of paper. He showed Zara how to fold them into paper boats. They took the boats outside and floated them in the puddles on the sidewalk. Zara laughed as her boat sailed all the way to the end of the street. It turned out to be a great day after all.

ELA Practice  ·  Grade 1

Reading Comprehension Practice

Nabila & Naviha  ·  Read the story, then answer the questions below

Read the story

Maya had a red balloon. She bought it at the store with her grandma. The balloon was bright and shiny. Maya held the string tight so it would not fly away.

On the way home, a strong wind blew. The string slipped from Maya's hand. The red balloon floated up, up, up into the sky. Maya watched until it was just a tiny dot.

Maya felt sad for a moment. But her grandma knelt down and said, "The balloon is on an adventure now." Maya smiled. She hoped the balloon was having fun.

Question 1 — What Happened?

What happened to Maya's balloon? Write 1–2 sentences.

Question 2 — How Did Maya Feel?

How did Maya feel at the end of the story? Why?

Question 3 — Make a Connection

Have you ever lost something? How did you feel? Write one sentence.
